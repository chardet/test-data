Welcome at 3D Gold Printing. We are a service bureau for jewellery designers. We manufacture your 3D designs using Direct Metal Laser Sintering technology.

Direct 3D Printing means no restrictions in your design. Since we don't use the so called "lost wax method", but Direct Metal Laser Sintering technology within 18 karats gold powder, your designs are without limitations.

Simply upload your computer design and we will send you a free price quotation.
